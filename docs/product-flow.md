# ClearVote Product Flow

## Purpose

ClearVote helps users identify potentially misleading political content, AI-generated material, and deepfakes while browsing the web. The goal is to provide a quick warning without interrupting the browsing experience and give users the option to learn more.

## User Flow

1. **User browses the web**

   * The user views online content normally.

2. **ClearVote identifies relevant content**

   * The browser extension detects political or other relevant content for analysis.

3. **Content is analyzed**

   * ClearVote checks for:

     * Misinformation or misleading claims
     * AI-generated content
     * Manipulated or deepfake media

4. **A small notification appears**

   * If a potential concern is detected, ClearVote displays a small, non-intrusive warning.

5. **Quick assessment is shown**

   * The notification may include:

     * Type of concern
     * Short credibility assessment
     * Confidence indicator, when appropriate
     * **Learn More** or **View Details** button

6. **User chooses whether to learn more**

   * Clicking **Learn More** opens the ClearVote website.

7. **Detailed results are displayed**

   * The website may show:

     * What information may be inaccurate
     * Why it was flagged
     * Additional context
     * Reliable sources
     * Related articles or information

8. **Digital well-being guidance**

   * ClearVote may provide short prompts encouraging users to verify information and think critically before reacting to or sharing content.

## Product Principle

ClearVote should inform users rather than make decisions for them. Assessments should clearly communicate uncertainty and provide supporting sources whenever possible.
